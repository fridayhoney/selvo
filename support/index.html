The switch will not stay on

Flip it again: Selvo picks another exit by itself. If it still fails, check that you are online and try again a little later.

iOS asks to add a configuration

That is the system prompt every app of this kind shows once. Allow it so Selvo can open its route.

How do I remove Selvo completely?

Delete the app. iOS removes its configuration with it.

Does Selvo cost anything?

No. Selvo is free and has no account.
