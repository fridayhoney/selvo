Support

The switch will not stay on

Flip it again: Selvo picks another exit by itself. If it still fails, check that you are online and try again a little later.

iOS asks to add a configuration

That is the system prompt every app of this kind shows once. Allow it so Selvo can open its route.

How do I remove Selvo completely?

Delete the app. iOS removes its configuration with it.

Does Selvo cost anything?

The first hours are free. After that, a one-time payment keeps the route open for a month or a year: no account, nothing renews by itself.

I paid and nothing changed

A payment usually shows up within five minutes; the app checks by itself. If it takes longer, open the chat from the app's menu (Help): we see which device writes and sort it out.

Something else?

Write to us in the chat. The menu of the app opens the same chat with your device already known to us.
