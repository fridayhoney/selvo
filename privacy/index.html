Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

No account

Selvo has no accounts. We never ask for your name, your email or your phone number.

What the app sends

When you flip the switch, the app asks our service for the list of exits and for permission to use one. That request carries a random identifier created on your device and, like any request on the internet, your network address. The identifier lets an exit accept your device. It says nothing about who you are.

Technical logs

The exits keep short-lived technical logs of connections: when, from which network address, to which address, with which identifier. They exist only to run the service, are rotated and discarded after a short time, and are never tied to a person. We do not inspect or store the content of your traffic, and we keep no browsing history.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking. There are no analytics, no ads and no trackers in the app.

Your traffic

While the switch is on, your traffic travels encrypted from your device to an exit we run, and from there to the internet. When the switch is off, Selvo touches nothing.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top tells you when it last changed.
