Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

No account

Selvo has no accounts. We never ask for your name, your email or anything else about you.

What the app sends

When you flip the switch, the app asks our service for the list of exits and for permission to use one. That request carries a random identifier created on your device and, like any request on the internet, your network address. The identifier lets an exit accept your device. It is not linked to you and is not shared with anyone.

The service keeps this identifier only as long as it takes to run the route, plus routine technical logs for a short time.

What we do not do

We keep no record of the sites you visit or of the content of your traffic. There are no analytics, no advertising and no trackers in the app.

Your traffic

While the switch is on, your traffic travels encrypted from your device to an exit we run, and from there to the internet. When the switch is off, Selvo touches nothing.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top tells you when it last changed.
