Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

No data collected

Selvo collects no personal data. There is no account, and we never ask for your name, your email or your phone number. We keep no browsing history, and there are no analytics, no ads and no trackers in the app.

Every connection is anonymous

When you flip the switch, the app asks our service for an exit and for permission to use it. The only thing that travels with that request is a random identifier created on your device. It says nothing about who you are, and it is never tied to a person.

Like every service on the internet, our exits see your network address while you are connected and keep routine technical logs for a short time. Those logs are discarded automatically and are never linked to anyone.

Nothing is shared

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking.

Your traffic

While the switch is on, your traffic travels encrypted from your device to an exit we run, and from there to the internet. We do not inspect or store its content. When the switch is off, Selvo touches nothing.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top tells you when it last changed.
